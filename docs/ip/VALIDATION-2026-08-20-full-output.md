## SERIOUS HOLES

- The Kohli roster was ALREADY cross-family — GPT-4o, Claude Sonnet 4.5, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Llama 4, Qwen3, Mistral Large 3, DeepSeek-V3, seven vendors. So model-family heterogeneity is not an untested lever; it is the specific intervention that was measured and found insufficient. That falsifies Caduceus DECISIONS.md #4 ('Heterogeneity by construction... same-family seats agree too easily') as a sufficient answer, and it means the shipped council IS the control condition, not the treatment.
- 1/ρ is the N→∞ asymptote, not a constant. N_eff = N/(1+(N−1)ρ) gives 1.68 at k=3, 1.95 at k=5 (the actual council), 2.18 at k=9, 2.52 at k=100. 'Regardless of headcount' is false in both directions, and '10,000 agents are worth ~3 opinions' is wrong on the thesis's own model — 10,000 buys ~52% more than 3. It is also the wrong statistic for detection tasks ('did anyone catch the bug?'), which are governed by union coverage over miss sets, not variance of a mean — and detection is what the whole verification thesis is about.
- Kohli's actual headline cuts AGAINST the product: 'the best single judge matches or outperforms the full panel across all conditions,' with established aggregation closing at most 11% of the gap. Citing that paper to justify building more mediated multi-agent infrastructure is citing 'don't use panels' as a reason to sell panels.
- Selective disclosure is priced as free upside and is not. By Krogh–Vedelsby, ensemble error = mean individual error − diversity. Starving each agent of evidence lowers ρ AND lowers individual competence p. Random Forests survive that trade only by averaging hundreds of weak trees; a five-seat council has no such budget, and below p=0.5 Condorcet runs backwards. The thesis never locates, or concedes the existence of, the optimum. 'Only lever' is also false — grounding one agent in execution (a test runner, a compiler, a live fetch) replaces a shared prior with an external oracle and decorrelates far more cheaply. His own believability ledger is that lever.
- Compartmentalization is sold as a benefit while the measured literature calls it a cost. Wu & Gong (2604.22879, Apr 2026) define context-fragmented violations — locally safe, collectively policy-violating — at 14–98% rates across eight frontier models, and conclude a centralized enforcement layer is required to compensate. That is a second component to budget, not a free property.
- Chunking is filter evasion, not defense. Real injections are sentence-sized and fit inside one chunk; the attacker controls length and structure; deterministic chunking is knowable and stable, which is strictly worse than randomized. Splitting a payload across independently-validated chunks plausibly IMPROVES evasion against per-chunk scanning while buying nothing at the terminal consumer — which is the node holding tools and write authority, the only node an attacker wants. Content-addressing plus multi-tenancy also creates a cross-tenant deduplication oracle (Harnik/Pinkas/Shulman-Peleg, IEEE S&P 2010).
- Items (3) and (4) are mutually exclusive on the same message. Non-relay fidelity means each recipient gets the original in full; compartmentalization means no recipient does. Two of five numbered pillars contradict each other, and neither the thesis nor any lens's research pass caught it.
- The proof's channel set excludes the channels that actually leak. Mediating MESSAGES does not mediate STATE: shared retrieval/vector index, shared object store, scratch filesystem, tool backends, caches, long-term memory, the model provider itself. Every real segmentation failure in regulated environments has gone through shared state. Note this is live in his own repo — caduceus/ledger.py is cross-seat, agent-influenced, and determines whose position leads the memo; that is an information flow between seats a message-graph proof declares impossible.
- The throughput concession is the wrong concession. Non-relay + chunking + manifest requires the mediator to hold the COMPLETE artifact before hashing and re-emitting — that kills token streaming and makes every hop store-and-forward, tens of seconds per hop at realistic token rates, compounding. The mediator is also STATEFUL and must be FAIL-CLOSED, so degradation is total outage, not slowdown — unlike a stateless sidecar, and unlike every mesh that deliberately splits control plane from data plane.
- The expressiveness tax is unmeasured and there is a public floor. CaMeL pays 84%→77% on AgentDojo for a strictly WEAKER constraint (it only prevents untrusted data from influencing control flow; it does not forbid text between components). 'What happens to task completion when you forbid free-form text?' is the first question any technical buyer asks and there is no number. Format restriction independently degrades reasoning (Tam et al., EMNLP 2024).
- The autonomy claim is double-booked. Supervision cost is converted from continuous labour into discrete labour plus a continuous RISK cost, and only the labour side is netted out. The thesis concedes unattended duration is a Goodhart risk multiplier and then sells unattended duration as the headline benefit. Policy count also is not constant — it scales as agent-types × data-classes × tenants × tool-scopes, and novel situations (the reason to run long) generate new policies. And the regulators are moving the other way: FINRA's 2026 Oversight Report asks where to place human-in-the-loop agent oversight; EU AI Act Art. 14 will require oversight by natural persons.

Every funded/acquired vendor in this category ships as a zero-code-change inline proxy — Invariant literally advertises 'without you having to change your code.' - **obvious hole:** Two, and the first is not a strategy problem, it is a representation problem. First: the thesis says "measured mean pairwise error correlation across LLM judges is ~0.391." Nobody measured it here. That is Kohli 2026's point estimate from three ChaosNLI datasets and one nine-model roster, and every downstream number in the claim — 2.6, 1/φ, chain-of-thought worsening things — is his too. Repeating that as an owned measurement in front of anyone who reads arXiv converts a defensible thesis into a credibility incident. Cite it or drop it. Second: the n_eff statistic is defined for k voters answering the SAME question, and it is being generalized to pipelines of agents doing DIFFERENT jobs. A retriever, a planner, a coder and a reviewer share no common error variable, so there is no φ to compute and "10,000 agents are worth ~3 opinions" is a category error against exactly the systems the architecture targets. Production multi-agent runs three to four specialized agents where each added role is worth roughly +7pp — the opposite regime from a redundant jury. Compounding both: the equicorrelation assumption that produces the "hard ceiling" is contradicted by the source paper's own pairwise range of [0.161, 0.603], meaning judge selection already beats the ceiling and has shipped as open code twice; and a paper published this month (arXiv:2608.06940) argues aggregate n_eff is specifically blind to where verification helps, showing +10.4 to +23.3pp conditional gains that the aggregate metric registered as −0.04 with a CI crossing zero. On the commercial question the lens was asked to settle: no. The correlation insight is markedly LESS sellable than the breaker architecture. It is published, open-sourced, reducible to fifty lines of Kish arithmetic, addressed to an ensemble size the market does not run, its honest recommendation is "buy less compute," and the category around it absorbed eight companies in fourteen months. It is a blog post that earns credibility and inbound, or a free CLI that seeds the architecture. It is not a company.
- **killer question:** Selective disclosure lowers φ by starving each agent of evidence — which also lowers each agent's individual accuracy p. Random Forests survive that trade only by averaging hundreds of weak trees; you have five expensive ones, and below p=0.5 Condorcet runs backwards. So: on your own council, on real settled runs, show me the measurement where the accuracy gained from the drop in φ exceeds the accuracy destroyed by the starvation, at k=5. If you cannot show it, the entire chain — correlation ceiling therefore selective disclosure therefore mandatory mediation therefore typed ports therefore static verification — has no load-bearing first link, and what remains is a prompt-injection architecture that Google and Microsoft already published.

### buyer-motion
- **verdict:** wounds
- **headline:** As of Aug 2026 the regulatory forcing function this thesis depends on has been explicitly switched OFF by the regulators themselves (SR 26-2, April 17 2026, excludes agentic AI from model risk scope and states non-compliance "will not result in supervisory criticism"; HIPAA Security Rule final action slipped to at least July 2027), so there is no budget line, no deadline, and no buyer — which means as a near-term revenue path this is a distraction, and its only honest near-term monetization is selling the METHOD as a fixed-scope consulting engagement, where $1,000 is 3-7 billable hours instead of 9-18 months.
- **obvious hole:** You built a compliance product for a compliance requirement that does not exist, and the regulators told you so four months ago.
