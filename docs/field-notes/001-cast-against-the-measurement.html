how_it_works

01

Question filed

It gets filed under a subject area, so the right track record applies.

02

Everyone answers alone

Every seat gets the same question at the same moment and writes without seeing anyone else.

03

Names taken off

Each seat then sees the others' answers with names and brands removed, and has to attack them.

04

Unexplained flips flagged

A seat that changes its mind without saying what changed it is named in the memo.

05

Scored, never voted

No show of hands. Each answer scores its seat's past record on this subject times how confident it said it was.

06

Lead and dissent both print

The top answer runs word for word, and the strongest disagreement runs beside it.

07

You settle it

When reality reports back you mark who was right. That grading is the only thing that moves a seat's standing.
